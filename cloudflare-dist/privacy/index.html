Privacy Policy

Mastiverse is committed to safeguarding your personal data in strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Effective Date / Last Updated: November 2026
🇮🇳 Applicable Jurisdiction: India
Compliance Notice: This document outlines Mastiverse's legal and operational practices for Indian jurisdiction. Certain bracketed terms (such as registered entity names or official grievance officer emails) are configurable placeholders awaiting final confirmation. Consult an Indian legal advisor before commercial deployment.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Mastiverse (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects personal data belonging to users (“Data Principals”) who access our casual browser gaming universe.

We operate as a Data Fiduciary under the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Information We Collect

In keeping with our commitment to privacy by design, Mastiverse strictly collects only the minimal data necessary to deliver instant browser gameplay and multiplayer leaderboards:

A. Guest Session Data

For users playing without creating an account, we generate a pseudonymous device identifier (e.g. usr_guest_xxxx) stored locally in your browser's localStorage to maintain your unlocked skins, game scores, and audio preferences. No email, telephone number, or real identity is collected for guest play.

B. Authenticated Profile Data

When you voluntarily sign in via Google Authentication or email signup, we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a unique Firebase user ID (UID).

C. Gameplay & Social Telemetry

High scores, session counts, achievements, friend links in “Your Squad”, and public leaderboard standing.

D. Technical & Diagnostic Logs

IP addresses, browser user-agent, operating system, and performance metrics collected via server logs to prevent DDoS attacks, mitigate botting, and ensure WebGL stability.

3. Legal Basis & Purpose of Processing

We process your personal data for specified, legitimate purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To authenticate your gaming session, host multiplayer lobbies, and sync leaderboards.
  • Fraud Prevention & Fair Play: To detect and prevent automated scripts, cheating, and malicious server exploitation.
  • Customer Support: To respond to bug tickets and user grievances submitted through our Contact portal.
  • Compliance: To satisfy statutory record-keeping obligations under applicable Indian laws.

4. Data Storage & Security Measures

User profiles and leaderboard records are securely hosted on Google Cloud Firebase Firestore infrastructure with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Strict Firebase Security Rules enforce that users can only modify their own authorized profile documents.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Mastiverse integrates with trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud authentication, Firestore database, and security rules.
  • Vercel Inc.: Global Edge CDN and application hosting.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Review our Third-Party Disclosure for full details.

6. Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023

As a Data Principal under Indian law, you possess enforceable rights:

Right to Access:You may request a summary of the personal data held about your account.
Right to Correction:You can update your username, avatar, and profile details anytime from your Profile page.
Right to Erasure:You have the right to request full deletion of your account and cloud data via our Account Deletion Portal.
Right to Grievance Redressal:You may submit privacy complaints to our Grievance Redressal Officer.

7. Children’s Privacy & Age Restrictions

Mastiverse does not knowingly process personal data of children under 18 years of age without verifiable parental consent in accordance with Section 9 of the DPDPA 2023. Our casual games feature no gambling, no predatory purchasing loops, and no adult content.

8. Data Retention Policy

We retain active user profile data for as long as your account remains open. Inactive guest telemetry stored in your browser localStorage can be cleared at any time by resetting your browser cookies and site data. Upon receiving a valid account deletion request, user records are deleted from Firestore within 30 days.

9. Grievance Redressal Officer (DPDPA 2023 & IT Rules 2021)

If you have questions, concerns, or grievances regarding our privacy practices, you may contact our designated Grievance Officer:
