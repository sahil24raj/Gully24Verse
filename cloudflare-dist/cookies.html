Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Mastiverse uses HTTP cookies, browser local storage, and session identifiers to power instant browser gameplay and preserve your stats.

Effective Date / Last Updated: November 2026
🇮🇳 Applicable Jurisdiction: India

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. In addition to standard HTTP cookies, modern web applications like Mastiverse utilize HTML5 Web Storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). These storage mechanisms allow your browser to remember your preferences (like sound mute states, active game high scores, and user avatars) between sessions without requiring server round-trips.

2. Technologies Used by Mastiverse

A. Strictly Essential Cookies

Necessary for the technical operation of the platform, including Firebase Auth session verification and CSRF protection.

B. Functional Local Storage

Stored under localStorage keys such as chill_arena_device_user_id and gullyverse_friends. These enable guest play, remember your chosen sound volume, preserve unlocked skins, and save high scores offline.

C. Performance & Security Cookies

Set by our CDN provider (Vercel) to balance server load, detect automated bot traffic, and optimize asset delivery.

3. Purposes of Cookie Usage

  • To keep you logged into your Google or email account.
  • To save your sound effects volume and mute preferences.
  • To verify fair-play scores during high-score submissions.
  • To cache 3D textures, WebGL assets, and audio sprites for lightning-fast 1-tap play.

4. Third-Party Cookies

When you use Google Sign-In, Google may set cookies on its authentication domain (accounts.google.com) to authenticate your Google profile securely. Mastiverse does not control Google's authentication cookies. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies or commercial tracking pixels.

5. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You can restrict or block cookies through your browser settings. However, please note that disabling essential cookies and local storage will reset your local game progress, logout active sessions, and restore default audio settings upon page refresh.

To clear Mastiverse local storage manually, open your browser Developer Tools (F12) > Application > Local Storage > Clear All.
