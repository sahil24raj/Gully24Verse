About GullyVerse

Bringing the thrill, banter, and nostalgia of Indian street games into an instant, zero-download digital arena.

Effective Date / Last Updated: November 2026
🇮🇳 Applicable Jurisdiction: India
“GullyVerse brings the excitement of gaming, competition, and desi culture together in one digital universe. From hostel rooms to local gullies, gaming has always been about community.”

1. Our Story

In every Indian neighborhood, the “gully” is where childhood legends are born. Whether it was betting a five-rupee coin on Book Cricket during boring lectures, dodging teachers in Chor Sipahi, or drifting an imaginary auto-rickshaw through chaotic traffic, desi games have their own distinct flavor of chaos, comedy, and skill.

However, modern mobile gaming became plagued by 10-gigabyte downloads, mandatory pay-to-win microtransactions, and aggressive ad spam. We asked a simple question: Why can't gaming be instant, fun, and culturally authentic?

GullyVerse was born out of this spirit. A high-performance WebGL and HTML5 gaming portal where you tap once and start playing in less than two seconds — on any phone, laptop, or low-spec browser. No installations. No waiting. Pure desi gaming.

3. The Desi Gaming Ecosystem

GullyVerse hosts a handcrafted lineup of 14+ instant games across 3D sports, racers, brawlers, and satirical party duels:

  • Turri Run 🛺: High-speed 3D auto-rickshaw drift racer through chaotic Chandni Chowk neon lanes.
  • Jelly Jalwa 🍬: Physics-based 3D 2048 runner with dynamic jelly squish mechanics.
  • Pehelwan Rush 🥊: 3D akhaada brawler where size equals power.
  • Chor Sipahi 👑: Classic 4-player hostel paper-chit game brought to real-time multiplayer.
  • Spin Cricket 🏏: The nostalgic school-day book cricket experience modernized for competitive leaderboards.
  • Indian Politics Arena ⚔️: Satirical battle royale featuring witty dialogue and political debates.
