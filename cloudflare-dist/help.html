2. Performance & Hardware Troubleshooting

Issue: Audio not playing on mobile tap

Modern mobile browsers require user interaction before playing audio. Tap anywhere on the game screen or click the sound icon in the top header to unlock Web Audio.

Issue: Low framerate / stuttering on laptops

Ensure you do not have power saver mode throttling your GPU. In Chrome, go to chrome://settings/system and toggle on “Use graphics acceleration when available”.
